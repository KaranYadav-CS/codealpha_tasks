# PhishGuard Demonstration Video Script
**CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship — Task 2: Phishing Awareness Training**
**Estimated Video Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds - 3 minutes  
**Format:** Screen recording with voiceover (webcam optional)

---

## 🎬 Video Overview & Presenter Setup
* **Screen Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full Screen Browser)
* **Browser:** Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
* **Starting Page:** `http://localhost:8000` (or local file path) with the page scrolled to the top in Dark Mode.
* **Tone:** Professional, articulate, clear, and confident cybersecurity engineering walkthrough.

---

## ⏱️ Video Script with Scene Directions

### Scene 1: Introduction & Project Overview (0:00 - 0:35)
**[Screen Action]:**
* Show the browser at the top of the **PhishGuard** homepage.
* Slowly move the mouse across the hero banner, brand title, and the live cyber threat statistics bar.
* Click the **Light Mode / Dark Mode** button once to demonstrate the seamless theme transition, then switch it back to Dark Mode.

**[Voiceover]:**
> "Hello everyone! My name is [Your Name], and I am a Cyber Security Intern at CodeAlpha.
> Today, I am proud to present my project for **Task 2: Phishing Awareness Training**—a web application named **PhishGuard Defense**.
>
> In cybersecurity, statistics show that over 90% of all organizational security breaches originate with a phishing or social engineering attack. 
> To address this critical human-factor risk, I engineered PhishGuard—a modern, lightweight, fully client-side cybersecurity training platform built using pure HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript.
>
> It runs completely locally with zero external dependencies, no databases, and zero tracking, making it lightweight, accessible, and 100% safe."

---

### Scene 2: Educational Curriculum & Core Lessons (0:35 - 1:05)
**[Screen Action]:**
* Smoothly scroll down to the **Phishing Awareness Curriculum** section.
* Click through a couple of category filter pills: "Attack Vectors", "Warning Signs", and "Verification & IR".
* Highlight one of the lesson cards, such as "Module 03: Anatomy of Warning Signs" and "Module 05: Technical Verification Protocols".

**[Voiceover]:**
> "Moving into the educational curriculum, PhishGuard features six comprehensive modules built strictly on standards from CISA, the FTC, and NIST.
> 
> We cover the end-to-end phishing lifecycle, analyze attack vectors from spear-phishing to emerging mobile threats like Smishing and Quishing, and unpack the cognitive psychology used by threat actors, such as manufactured urgency and authority pretexting.
>
> We also break critical industry misconceptions—for example, teaching learners why the HTTPS padlock does not mean a website is safe, and explaining how to parse root domains versus subdomains."

---

### Scene 3: The Interactive Threat Inspector (1:05 - 1:45)
**[Screen Action]:**
* Scroll down to the **Realistic Threat Inspector** section.
* Show **Scenario 1 (Microsoft 365 Email)**.
* Click on **Hotspot #1 (Sender Display Name)** in the sidebar or directly in the mockup.
* Point to the dynamic deep-dive analysis card on the right showing the critical severity tag, threat mechanism, and defensive verification protocol.
* Click on **Hotspot #3 (Deceptive Hyperlink)** to show the mismatch between button text and the actual destination URL (`portal-auth-credential.invalid`).
* Switch to **Scenario 2 (Banking Smishing)** to showcase the mobile smartphone frame and discuss SMS phishing lures.

**[Voiceover]:**
> "Now let's examine the centerpiece of the training: the **Interactive Threat Inspector**.
>
> Rather than just lecturing, PhishGuard allows users to inspect simulated, harmless threat scenarios. 
> Here in Scenario 1, we see a convincing corporate Microsoft 365 password expiration notice. 
> When I click on the sender address hotspot, the system immediately highlights the display name spoofing and explains why an external domain is a major red flag.
>
> When I inspect the call-to-action button, learners can clearly see the underlying link directs to an unauthorized credential-harvesting domain.
>
> We can also switch seamlessly to our mobile Smishing scenario, rendered inside a realistic smartphone interface to demonstrate how cybercriminals exploit smaller mobile screens and panic triggers like fraudulent wire alerts."

---

### Scene 4: Pre-Interaction Safety Checklist & Risk Meter (1:45 - 2:10)
**[Screen Action]:**
* Scroll to the **Interactive Verification Checklist** section.
* Check off the first 3 items: show the meter fill up and the verdict card switch to "Moderate Risk (Caution Required)".
* Check the remaining items to reach 6/6: show the meter reach 100% and transition to the green "Low Probability of Phishing" state.
* Click the "Reset Checklist" button to demonstrate the reset functionality.

**[Voiceover]:**
> "Next, we have the **Interactive Verification Checklist**. 
> This provides users with a practical 6-point decision framework before they click links, open attachments, or input credentials.
>
> As users check off verified criteria—such as checking the sender domain, absence of urgency, and inspecting file extensions—the dynamic safety readiness meter calculates the threat probability in real time, advising users when to stop, isolate, or perform Out-of-Band verification."

---

### Scene 5: Interactive 10-Question Phishing Quiz (2:10 - 2:45)
**[Screen Action]:**
* Scroll to the **Interactive Phishing Defense Quiz** section.
* Answer Question 1 (e.g. Option B): show the instant green checkmark and the educational explanation box. Click **Next Question**.
* Answer an incorrect choice on a question to show the red cross feedback and explanation.
* Quickly advance through to the final question and click **Finish & View Results**.
* Show the Results card: point out the final score (e.g., 9/10), accuracy percentage (90%), the "Elite Cyber Defender" badge, and scroll briefly through the detailed question review list.
* Click **🔄 Retake Quiz & Reset** to show that it cleanly resets to Question 1.

**[Voiceover]:**
> "To validate learning retention, I built a fully functional 10-question evaluation quiz engine.
>
> Each question presents a realistic workplace scenario. When an option is selected, the application immediately evaluates the choice, providing instant visual feedback and a comprehensive security takeaway for every single option.
>
> Upon completion, learners receive a detailed performance summary with their exact accuracy rate, a competency badge—such as 'Elite Cyber Defender'—and a question-by-question breakdown. Learners can also reset and retake the quiz at any time."

---

### Scene 6: Incident Runbook, References & Conclusion (2:45 - 3:00)
**[Screen Action]:**
* Scroll through the **Post-Incident Response Runbook** (highlighting the 5 emergency steps: Isolate, Rotate, Revoke, Notify, Report).
* Briefly show the **References & Standards** section linking to CISA, FTC, and NIST.
* Return to the top of the page.

**[Voiceover]:**
> "Finally, we provide a 5-step emergency incident response runbook for anyone who might have clicked a link or entered credentials, alongside verified citations to CISA, the FTC, and NIST SP 800-61.
>
> This complete platform is documented in our repository with a comprehensive README, reference citations, and verification guides.
>
> Thank you so much for watching, and thank you to CodeAlpha for this rewarding cybersecurity internship experience!"

## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording
1. **Clear Audio:** Use a headset or external microphone if available.
2. **Smooth Mouse Movements:** Avoid rapid or erratic cursor movements; keep the focus deliberate.
3. **Pacing:** Speak at a steady, conversational pace.
4. **Browser Zoom:** 100% or 110% zoom ensures all text and mockups are crisp on 1080p video.
